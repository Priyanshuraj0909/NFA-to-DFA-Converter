Exhaustive check

            
Runs every string over Σ up to the given length on the NFA and on the DFA and reports any disagreement.

            

              
              
            

            

          

          

            
Exact equivalence proof

            
Breadth-first search over all reachable pairs (NFA state-set, DFA state). L(NFA) = L(DFA) iff no reachable pair disagrees on acceptance; otherwise the shortest counterexample is shown.
